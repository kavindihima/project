Vehicle Terms and Conditions

01. Under these tax regulations, the manner in which the vehicle is used is subject to the payment of applicable rental charges by the vehicle custodian.

02. The vehicle is bound to be used according to the specific instructions provided for its intended use.

03. All charges, including meter charges, are the responsibility of the vehicle custodian, as noted on the vehicle's meter.

04. In case of inconvenience or vehicle-related issues, the vehicle custodian is responsible for payment after a prior notice.

05. It is not the responsibility of the vehicle custodian to deliver the vehicle to other institutions or to hand it over to another person without approval, and the custodian will be required to pay rental charges upon return to the original condition.

06. While using the vehicle, the vehicle custodian is responsible for its proper maintenance in accordance with the agreed standards.

07. If any dues remain, it is the responsibility of the custodian to settle them before the agreement is finalized.

08. In case of any damage or accident involving the vehicle, the vehicle custodian will bear the associated costs and responsibilities for repairs.

09. If the vehicle requires repairs, any additional costs incurred during the repair process will be charged to the custodian.

10. Once the payment for rental charges is made, the vehicle custodian remains responsible for the use of the vehicle.

11. A penalty of Rs. 300,000 or more may be imposed in cases of breaches as stipulated in the agreement.

12. If any violations related to vehicle usage are found, they must be paid for by the vehicle custodian in the previous time frame.

13. The vehicle custodian is not released from responsibility for damages caused by unauthorized actions, even if instructed otherwise.

14. If the vehicle is used for any specific purpose, it must be driven in accordance with the manufacturer's guidelines or the instructions provided for other vehicles.

15. The vehicle custodian must ensure to follow the authorized guidelines for vehicle usage at all times.

16. If any damage occurs due to unauthorized use during the vehicle's operation, the custodian is responsible for paying for the damages as compensation.

17. While using the vehicle, the custodian must generally adhere to the maintenance procedures as prescribed.

18. The custodian is responsible for ensuring that any maintenance needs of the vehicle are fully met during its usage.
